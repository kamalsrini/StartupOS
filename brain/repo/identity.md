# Identity

**UnitOne.ai** — enterprise AI agent runtime security.

## Products
- **Sentinel** — runtime behavioral security for AI agents: drift detection, blast-radius scoring, and instrumentation of every tool call a long-running agent makes while it holds company credentials.
- **IQstudio** — secure enterprise vibe-coding platform: agentic fixes (fixer + eval path), design-tier threat modeling (/spine), multi-agent review with verification.

## Thesis
A long-running agent holding real credentials is UnitOne's own use case. StartupOS (the internal founder operating system) ships instrumented by Sentinel from day one; the instrumentation is also a product story.

## What we are not
Not a chat wrapper, not a generic SOC tool. Evidence over narrative: teardowns, scan results, and runtime traces are the sales material.
