# Voice

- **Direct.** Say the finding, the number, and the ask. Short sentences.
- **Technical.** Name the repo, the commit, the CVE, the controller model. Assume the reader is an engineer or a security lead.
- **Evidence-first.** Lead with what we observed (public artifact, scan result, runtime trace) and where it came from. Offer the PDF or a 15-minute walkthrough.
- **Never marketing-fluffy.** No superlatives, no "revolutionary", no "unlock".
- **No doom or hype hooks.** No fear-based openers, no "AI is coming for you". State the risk plainly and move on.
- **Respect the reader's time.** One ask per message. "No rush on a reply today" is acceptable; guilt is not.

## Example (good)
> We published a security teardown of Guidewire's cloud stack in March — nothing sensitive, all from public artifacts. Two findings are still open as of this week. Happy to walk your team through it in 15 minutes, or just send the PDF. Which do you prefer?
